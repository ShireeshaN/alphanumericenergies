Why On-Grid

A practical fit for working facilities.

On-grid solar is especially suited to sites with active daytime demand, available roof or site area and an existing utility connection.

01

Solar during operating hours

Generated energy can directly support connected commercial or industrial loads.

02

Grid support remains available

The utility network continues to support the facility whenever solar output is below demand.

03

Scalable around the facility

Capacity and architecture can be structured around the site's usable area and electrical profile.

04

Connected performance visibility

Monitoring supports ongoing visibility into generation, operating status and system trends.
