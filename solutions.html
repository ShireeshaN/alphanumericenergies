Solutions overview

Three solution paths. Each with a clear role.

Choose the solar model that fits the site, continuity expectation and scale requirement. The structure below helps explain each pathway in a crisp and premium way.

On-GridOff-GridGround-Mounted
01 / On-Grid

Grid-synced solar for active facilities.

Ideal for rooftops and operating sites where utility power is available and daytime solar can reduce dependence on conventional supply.

  • Commercial and industrial rooftops
  • Net-metering compatible strategy
  • Minimal storage requirement
Open page
02 / Off-Grid

Independent power with storage continuity.

Designed for locations where energy autonomy matters — remote properties, backup-critical sites or places with limited grid confidence.

  • Battery-backed power flow
  • Autonomous operation logic
  • Higher resilience in low-grid conditions
Open page
03 / Ground-Mounted

Higher-scale generation on open land.

Suitable for larger energy requirements where ground space allows organized array planning, maintenance access and future expansion.

  • Larger capacity potential
  • Planned row spacing and orientation
  • Utility, campus and industrial use
